Use concise, direct copy for the mobility surfaces:

- say what happened
- say what remains saved
- say what the user can do next
- say whether money was charged or refunded
- say whether the ride is still active
- say when support or safety escalation is required

Avoid internal engineering terms in user-facing copy.
Use localized message keys in implementation and render clear fallback text in the UI.
