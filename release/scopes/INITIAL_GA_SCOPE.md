# Initial GA scope

This scope is intentionally narrow and product-specific.

The selected launch geography is:

- Australia
- Melbourne, Victoria

NovaID is limited to workforce, rider, driver, agent, and merchant identities with password authentication, session lifecycle, tenant-aware authorization, audit events, revocation, and account recovery.

NovaPay is limited to domestic AUD flows with controlled funding, controlled merchant payment, refunds, reversals, receipts, and reconciliation. Live money movement remains disabled unless licensed provider approval and live credentials exist.

NovaRide is limited to pre-approved riders and drivers in Werribee, Wyndham Vale, Tarneit, and Melton with registration, identity verification through approved NovaID flows, request/dispatch/acceptance/trip lifecycle, cancellation, safety event reporting, support, receipt generation, and controlled cash or approved NovaPay payment.
