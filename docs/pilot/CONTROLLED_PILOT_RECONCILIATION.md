# Controlled Pilot Reconciliation

Reconciliation is required for cash, wallet, QR, and fare-settlement activity.

Rules:
- opening float is recorded
- every cash-in and cash-out is ledgered
- ride cash payments are recorded in the controlled-pilot ledger
- end-of-day reconciliation is mandatory
- shortages and overages are flagged
- receipts and audit evidence must match the ledger

NovaPay owns the financial ledger.
NovaTrust records the evidence package.
