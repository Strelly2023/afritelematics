# Controlled Pilot Operations Runbook

## Operating model

- Only invited pilot users may access pilot surfaces.
- Devices must be approved and bound.
- Monitoring, support, and incident response stay enabled throughout the pilot.
- Rollback criteria must be defined before any pilot session starts.

## Daily checks

- Verify environment and registry state.
- Confirm support coverage and escalation contacts.
- Confirm monitoring dashboards and logs are healthy.
- Confirm APK distribution is still controlled-only.

## Session checks

- Verify identity and device approval.
- Verify transaction limits and geography.
- Confirm receipts and evidence are generated.
- Confirm no live payment path is active unless approved.
