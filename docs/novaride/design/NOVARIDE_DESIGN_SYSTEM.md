# NovaRide design system

The supplied master artwork establishes deep navy, electric cyan, emerald green and white as the core palette. It communicates movement, routing, technology and trust. Rider and driver manifests now share the same SHA-256-pinned asset (`456117631c687fbd37d244a259ed08bc637120935de723cdd6f07314cd7d0862`).

Interfaces should preserve high contrast, minimum 44-point touch targets, plain status language, visible focus, screen-reader labels, non-colour status cues, reduced-motion support, and clear offline/loading/error/retry states. Safety actions remain persistent and visually distinct from routine actions.
