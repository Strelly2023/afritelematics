# AFRIPAY_INVESTOR_NARRATIVE

AfriPay is a cryptographic financial protocol designed to produce verifiable evidence, not just assertions. Investors can inspect the same exported proof bundles, public verification portal, regulator package, and independent verifier outputs that auditors use.

Why it matters:
- trust is reduced through cryptography
- evidence is reproducible
- verification is independent of operator access
- integration evidence is portable across counterparties
