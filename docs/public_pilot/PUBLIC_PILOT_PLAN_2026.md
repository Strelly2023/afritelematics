# Public Pilot Plan 2026

## Scope

Public Pilot permits approved external users, approved regions, and limited real payments under governed limits.

## Boundaries

- GA remains disabled.
- Unrestricted signup remains disabled.
- Limited real payments require explicit approval.
- NovaID owns identity verification.
- NovaPay executes financial services.
- NovaRide owns mobility only.
- NovaTrust records evidence.
- NovaAI remains advisory only.

## Entry Gates

- Configured `PUBLIC_PILOT` environment.
- Approved-user registry in place.
- Geography restrictions in place.
- Monitoring, incident response, rollback, support, and reconciliation documented.
- PRR remains blocked until exit criteria pass.
