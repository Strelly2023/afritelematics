# Public Pilot Users

## Participant Groups

- 10 real drivers.
- 30 real riders.
- 2 real merchants.
- 3 real agents.
- 2 real businesses.

## Participation Rule

- Participants are invitation-based and restricted to the approved cohort.
- NovaID verification and device trust are required.
- Approved devices only.
- NovaTrust records receipts, audit evidence, and replay evidence.
