# Public Pilot Payment Policy

- Payment mode: PILOT_REAL.
- Maximum transaction amount: AUD 50.
- Daily limit: AUD 200.
- Monthly limit: AUD 1,000.
- Limited real payments are allowed under pilot governance.
- Live charging remains separately gated by explicit approval.
- NovaPay owns payment execution.
- NovaRide hands off fares to NovaPay and does not execute provider logic.
- NovaTrust evidence is required for payment records.
