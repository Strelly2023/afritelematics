# Public Pilot Operational Readiness

## Readiness Controls

- Production infrastructure may be used, but public launch is not allowed.
- Approved pilot regions only.
- Transaction limits enforced.
- Manual review required for high-risk identity cases.
- Reconciliation and support are mandatory.

## Operational Owners

- NovaRide operations for dispatch, safety, and trip integrity.
- NovaPay operations for limited real payments, refunds, and reconciliation.
- NovaID operations for onboarding, verification, and audit trail.
- NovaTrust for evidence generation and replayability.
