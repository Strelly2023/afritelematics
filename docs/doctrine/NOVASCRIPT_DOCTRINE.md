# NovaScript Doctrine

NovaScript is proof-governed engineering intelligence infrastructure. It helps organizations generate, analyze, govern, verify, assure, certify, and exchange software trust artifacts.

## Authority Stack

```text
Truth Authority:
Proof

Governance Authority:
Policy Registry

Certification Authority:
NovaTrust CA

Federation Authority:
Trust Exchange

Audit Authority:
External Verification

Presentation Authority:
Reports
```

## Doctrine

NovaScript does not claim truth from narrative, model confidence, or presentation output. Truth is carried by verifiable proof artifacts, deterministic receipts, certificate chains, assurance reports, evidence hashes, and federation exchange records.

## Boundary

NovaScript builds trust artifacts and engineering intelligence. It does not override NovaProgramming runtime governance, mutate production systems without approval, or replace external legal audit authority.
