# NCP-006B Admin Guide

Date: 2026-07-18

## Responsibilities

Design administrators manage:

- design workspaces
- access and approvals
- baselines and supersession
- traceability and coverage
- imports, exports, and handoffs
- retention and legal hold controls

## Operational notes

- Treat all design artifacts as versioned and auditable.
- Do not approve synthetic research as primary research.
- Do not bypass accessibility or localization gates.
- Use the portal and API; do not mutate authoritative records directly.

## External blockers

Live browser certification, live provider verification, and live Figma integration are not available in this environment.
