# NCP-006B Review, Approval, and Baseline Governance

Date: 2026-07-18

Design content moves through review, approval, baseline, implementation-readiness, and historical preservation states.

Policy gates require:

- authorized reviewer role
- no unresolved blocking findings
- no unverified AI-generated research masquerading as primary research
- required requirement and architecture baselines
- traceability and evidence preservation

Approved baselines are immutable. New content versions require new review and approval.
