# Controlled Pilot Test Framework

## What it means

Controlled Pilot is a limited, invited-user validation stage after Private Development and Internal QA and before general availability.

## Allowed

- Approved users only.
- Approved devices only.
- Approved operators only.
- Limited geography.
- Limited transaction amounts.
- Simulated payments by default.
- Cash through documented pilot procedure.

## Blocked

- Public launch.
- Unrestricted signup.
- Unapproved devices or users.
- Production credentials.
- Live charging without approval.
- External payouts without approval.

## Validation focus

- Access control.
- Device control.
- Payment safety.
- NovaRide mobility.
- NovaPay execution.
- NovaID identity.
- NovaTrust evidence.
- Monitoring and incident response.

## Before GA

- Controlled pilot tests pass.
- Exit criteria are satisfied.
- No critical blocker remains.
- A formal rollback path exists.
